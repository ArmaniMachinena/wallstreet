Lexicon

    Microfinance

    • PAR30 — loans with a payment >30 days late ÷ gross portfolio.
    • OSS — operating revenue ÷ (financial + operating + loan-loss expense). >100% covers costs.
    • Portfolio yield — interest & fees ÷ average portfolio; compare with cost of funds.
    • Funding stack — deposits, DFI loans, MIV debt, local bonds, social bonds.
